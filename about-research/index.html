Publications

Transformers as Provable Approximators of Sparse Principal Component Analysis
Qinyan Liu, Yan Chen, and Canhong Wen

STAI-X 2026 Strong Accept (with one of the five highest average review scores)
[code]

Advisor: Prof. Canhong Wen, School of Management Sciences, USTC

  • Extended the theoretical understanding of Transformers to fully unsupervised statistical learning, using sparse PCA as a canonical high-dimensional problem.

  • Established a constructive approximation framework showing that ReLU Transformers can emulate truncated power iterations, with error guarantees under a noisy spiked covariance model and necessary assumptions.

  • Derived high probability statistical error bounds under standard learning-theoretic assumptions and an additional restricted sparse eigengap conditions, quantifying the expressiveness-capacity tradeoff.

  • Validated the method on high-dimensional genomic datasets and noisy synthetic datasets. Results demonstrate that unsupervised Transformers can achieve SPCA performance comparable to baselines and efficient batched inference.
