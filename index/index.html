About me

I am a rising senior at School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), majoring in Mathematics with a concentration in Probability Theory and Mathematical Statistics.

I am applying for Ph.D. positions starting in Fall 2027. My research focuses on statistical machine learning, with particular interests in the theoretical understanding of Transformers, diffusion models, high-dimensional statistics, and generative modeling. My current work includes hyperbolic generative models for hypergraphs and Transformer-based SPCA.
